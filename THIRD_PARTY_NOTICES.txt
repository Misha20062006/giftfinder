The Android source package includes the build recipe and Qt binding patches.
It can be rebuilt with replacement dynamically linked Qt/PySide libraries and
signed with your own key for a separate installation. The original release
signing key is private and is not required to modify or rebuild the source.

Android 0.7.1 regenerates and relinks the thin Shiboken extension from the
same Qt 6.12.0 source, commit f50facb83fc3dc5f3240c854668c7a6dd93871b2:
https://github.com/qtproject/pyside-pyside-setup/tree/f50facb83fc3dc5f3240c854668c7a6dd93871b2
Its original LGPL/GPL terms remain applicable; this does not relicense Qt code.
Pinned source checksums and linker flags: mobile/android-build-inputs.json.
Rebuild implementation: mobile/android_runtime.py.

Additional bundled model previews for the reviewed themes are Telegram gift
artwork retrieved through Gift Changes. URLs and file hashes are recorded in
giftfinder/preview_seed/SOURCES-new-themes.json; artwork is not under Unlicense.
